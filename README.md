# Cosmic Whale Village — Coolest Art

A majestic giant celestial whale swimming through a vibrant cosmic nebula,
carrying a small glowing lantern-lit village on its back.

The whale's body is adorned with bioluminescent patterns in cyan and magenta.
Stars and colorful nebula clouds in deep purple, pink and teal swirl around.
A huge glowing full moon hangs in the background.

## How it works

`main.py` copies `art.png` (1024×683, 367 KB) to `/output/entry.png`.
Deterministic: the same bytes are written on every run.

## Files

- `main.py` — the entry program
- `art.png` — the artwork (PNG, 1024×683)
